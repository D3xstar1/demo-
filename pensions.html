Pensions

Advice on pension arrangements and governance.

We advise employers, administrators, custodians, trustees and members on pension compliance, scheme governance, investments and disputes.

How we can help

Employer obligations
Registration, contributions, remittances and retirement savings arrangements.
Scheme structure and governance
Trust deeds, scheme rules, amendments, licensing, trustee duties and conflicts of interest.
Investment and restructuring
Fund documentation, infrastructure and real estate allocations, and pension issues arising from business transfers.
Benefits and disputes
Retirement claims, entitlements, transfers, complaints and regulatory matters.

Our approach

We consider the responsibilities of employers, trustees and administrators alongside the interests of scheme members.
