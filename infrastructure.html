Infrastructure

Advice from project concept through operation.

We work with sponsors, contractors, lenders and public authorities on planning, financing, procurement and delivery of infrastructure projects in Nigeria.

How we can help

Public-private partnerships
Project structuring, bid support, concessions, procurement and negotiation with public authorities.
Project finance
Facility agreements, security, guarantees and intercreditor arrangements for sponsors and lenders.
Construction contracts
FIDIC and bespoke contracts, EPC arrangements, variations, delay claims and cost claims.
Land, permits and operation
Site acquisition, rights of way, environmental approvals, operations agreements and long-term service contracts.

Our approach

We look at how risks are allocated over the life of the project, from procurement and construction to financing and operation.
